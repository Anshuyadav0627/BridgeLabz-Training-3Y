About Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan. It is also known as the Pink City.

Famous Places

Hawa Mahal, Amer Fort and City Palace are famous places in Jaipur.

Famous Food

Jaipur is famous for Dal Baati Churma, Kachori and Ghewar.

Why Visit Jaipur?

Jaipur has beautiful forts, palaces, markets and rich culture.
